I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I pursue a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present) under the supervision of Professor Junxian He. I previously completed my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), receiving my B.Eng. in June 2024 (2020-2024). Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies.

My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML). My research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and Interpretability

During my studies, I have worked as a research intern at several organizations:

- **Research Intern**, MINIMAX (February 2025 - Present)
- **Research Intern**, Tencent WXG (June 2024 - September 2024), advised by Zifei Shan
- **Research Intern**, Shanghai AI Lab (June 2023 - December 2023), advised by Prof. Yu Cheng

I received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
